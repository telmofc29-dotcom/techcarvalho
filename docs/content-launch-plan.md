# Content launch plan — first ~100 articles

A prioritised plan, not a manifest — working titles here are planning
targets, not yet-written content. A parallel research/writing pass may
already be producing some of these; treat overlap as confirmation the
priority was right, not a conflict. Every piece, when actually written,
follows the standing editorial rules: no fabricated testing/ownership, no
rumour presented as fact, real sourcing for any factual claim, plain
hedged language ("According to X...") wherever a claim isn't Tech
Carvalho's own.

Columns: **Type** (review/guide/comparison/news/troubleshooting) ·
**Intent** (informational/commercial/transactional/navigational) ·
**Ever/Time** (evergreen vs time-sensitive).

## Notes for whoever picks this up next

- Camera-specific titles above assume the Canon catalogue research
  (parallel to this doc) lands roughly the products named — verify slugs
  exist before linking `linkedProducts` in the actual `ContentImport`.
- Tier 3 titles are intentionally vague on specifics ("what's confirmed
  so far") because writing them requires research at actual publish time,
  not now — do not backfill these with today's training-data memory.
- Category slugs referenced informally above (Cameras & Photography,
  Astrophotography, Drones & FPV, Action Cameras, Computing, Networking,
  Gaming) match `PLANNED_CATEGORIES` in `src/lib/public/categories.ts` —
  verify against the real `taxonomy_categories` table before import.
